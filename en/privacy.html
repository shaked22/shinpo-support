Privacy Policy

Shinpo is a free productivity app published by Shaked Tayar for people aged 13 and over. This policy covers the app, widgets, Live Activities, support and public information pages. Contact: shinpo.kai22@gmail.com.

Your content

Shinpo processes the tasks, notes, images, folders, habits, completion history, countdowns, dates and preferences you choose to enter. Providing content is voluntary; the corresponding feature cannot work without it. This information is used to save and display your content, calculate progress, schedule requested reminders and synchronize your devices. You can view, correct and delete content in the app. There is no separate Shinpo account or password.

Device storage and iCloud

Content is stored on your device. When available and permitted by your Apple settings, Apple's CloudKit synchronizes it with your private iCloud database and devices using the same Apple Account. The publisher has no app-content server and cannot browse that private database in the developer console. Apple operates iCloud under its own terms and privacy policy. Connectivity, storage and device settings affect synchronization. Deletions can take time to reach other devices, particularly while offline.

Permissions and screen privacy

Notifications are scheduled locally after your permission. Widgets receive a local content snapshot. Widgets and Live Activities can expose titles on your screen. Use Hide sensitive content in Shinpo settings and iOS notification and Lock Screen controls. The system photo picker supplies only images you select. Export files may include private content and images: store and share them carefully.

Information received by the publisher

This release has no advertising, tracking SDK, third-party analytics SDK or paid features. It does not send your productivity content to the publisher. Apple may separately provide aggregate App Store statistics, reviews, crash reports or TestFlight feedback according to your Apple settings and its terms. Email support uses Gmail, operated by Google: we receive your address, message, attachments and message metadata. Send only necessary information; do not send passwords, full exports or private information about others.

Website and service providers

The public information pages use GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security and processes service information under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. These pages add no advertising scripts, analytics scripts, contact forms or cookies of their own. External links lead to services with their own policies. Apple, Google and GitHub may process information outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention, export and deletion

Content remains on your devices and in private iCloud until you remove it. Settings → Legal & Privacy offers local JSON export and Delete all app data. Export contains records downloaded to the device: allow sync to finish first. Delete all removes that content and submits deletions to iCloud when available, and clears local notifications, widget data and Live Activities. Leave the app online so deletions can propagate. Uninstalling alone may leave an iCloud copy; manage it in Apple Account storage settings. Agreement records stay locally until cleared or the app is uninstalled. Support messages are reviewed for deletion no later than 12 months after resolution, except information still needed for an ongoing request, security issue, legal duty or dispute; exceptions are reviewed periodically.

Your rights and choices

Email shinpo.kai22@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, or raise a privacy concern. We handle requests and verify identity only as reasonably necessary under applicable law. The publisher cannot retrieve or remotely erase private iCloud content; use the in-app data tools or Apple storage settings. You may stop app use, disable Shinpo's iCloud access in Apple settings and remove your content. Acceptance does not waive privacy or consumer rights. This release does not collect consent to future advertising, analytics or marketing.

Teenagers and children

Shinpo is intended for ages 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not request a date of birth, identity document or separate age declaration. If you believe a child under 13 sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it where required. Accepting the app's terms is not age verification or verified parental consent.

Security and changes

The app uses Apple's storage and private iCloud protections. Protect your device and Apple Account; no system guarantees absolute security. Report security issues to shinpo.kai22@gmail.com without accessing anyone else's data. Material changes to data purposes or recipients will be explained before changed processing begins, with any required new choice or consent. This policy and its version remain available in the app and on the website.
