iCloud and reminders

Use the same Apple Account on each device, enable iCloud Drive and Shinpo in Apple settings, check available storage and leave the app open briefly with a working connection. The publisher cannot access or restore your private iCloud database. Check Settings → Notifications → Shinpo on each device for reminder permissions.

Export and deletion

Open Settings → Legal & Privacy to export a local JSON copy or delete app data. Export includes records already downloaded. Deletion is permanent and can propagate through iCloud; keep the app online. Uninstalling alone may leave an iCloud copy. The existing-data option on the welcome screen provides data controls without accepting updated terms; opening it accesses the store and may synchronize existing content with private iCloud.

Contacting us

Include the app version, device model, iOS version and steps leading to the issue. Remove private content from screenshots. Do not send passwords, Apple Account credentials, full exports or identity documents. Use Privacy, Accessibility or Security in the subject where relevant. Requests are handled according to their nature and applicable obligations; we do not promise one fixed response time for all general support questions.

Apple services

For Apple Account or iCloud billing issues, contact Apple Support. Shinpo currently sells no subscription or Premium upgrade. Do not send payment information or Apple passwords to the support address.
